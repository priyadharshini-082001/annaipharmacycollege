Zero Tolerance Towards Ragging

Annai College of Pharmacy is committed to providing a safe, respectful and inclusive environment for every student.

✓ ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY
ANNAI COLLEGE OF PHARMACY Safe Campus.
Respectful Community.

OUR COMMITMENT

The institution follows a zero-tolerance policy towards ragging and acts in accordance with the applicable regulations and guidelines of the competent authorities.

01 / PREVENTION

Our Objectives

STUDENT SAFETY Building a
Respectful Campus
01

Prevent ragging in any form on the College campus and hostel, ensuring that every student can learn and live in a safe, respectful and supportive environment.

02

Create awareness among students about anti-ragging laws, institutional policies, responsibilities and the consequences of participating in or encouraging ragging.

03

Promote discipline, dignity, mutual respect and professional behaviour among students while encouraging responsible conduct throughout campus life.

04

Encourage healthy interaction between students across different years and create a welcoming atmosphere based on cooperation, friendship and mutual understanding.

05

Ensure that complaints or concerns related to ragging receive prompt and appropriate attention through the designated College authorities and Anti-Ragging Committee.

06

Maintain a safe, inclusive and professional educational environment where every student can pursue academic and personal development without fear, intimidation or harassment.
