College Profile

Shaping Competent and Compassionate Pharmacy Professionals

Annai College of Pharmacy Harur, Dharmapuri

Annai College of Pharmacy, Harur, was established in 2023 under Annai Educational Trust with a commitment to quality pharmaceutical education. The College seeks to nurture competent, ethical and innovative pharmacy professionals who can contribute meaningfully to patient care, public health, research, industry and society. The institution is approved by the Pharmacy Council of India, New Delhi, affiliated to The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University, Chennai, and recognized by the Government of Tamil Nadu. Its academic programmes and institutional practices are aligned with the applicable standards and regulations of the competent authorities. The campus in Harur, Dharmapuri District, offers a modern learning environment with smart classrooms, advanced laboratories, a Central Library, E-Library, computer and internet facilities, a Drug Museum, Drug Information Centre, Machine Room and Central Instrumentation Room.

About Us

Annai College of Pharmacy, Harur, is committed to providing quality pharmacy education through academic excellence, practical training, research, innovation, and ethical values. We prepare students to become competent healthcare professionals for the pharmaceutical industry and society.
